On time by mode (per trip)

2 hours

Each trip counted once, where TfNSW measures it: buses as they leave their first stop (Greater Sydney Bus Contract: 59 s early to 5 min 59 s late), other modes by the predicted delay at their final stop (TfNSW punctuality: within 5 min, 6 min intercity). Trips with no live prediction aren't counted; cancelled ones count as not on time. Buses starting outside the model aren't seen leaving their first stop, so they aren't counted.
